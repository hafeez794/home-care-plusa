HOME CARE PLUSA WEBSITE
=======================

1. This is a static, mobile-friendly website.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview it.
3. Before publishing, open script.js and replace:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "92XXXXXXXXXX";
   with your real WhatsApp number, e.g. 923001234567 (no + or spaces).
4. Upload the extracted folder containing index.html, style.css and script.js to Netlify Drop.
5. The booking form opens WhatsApp. It does not store patient information on a server.

IMPORTANT:
- Confirm service availability, staff credentials, prices and operating hours before publishing.
- The website does not provide emergency medical care or diagnose/treat patients.
- Add your real phone number, exact service area and business details before going live.
