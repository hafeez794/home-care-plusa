Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our home healthcare services.

Are home healthcare services available 24/7?

You can contact Home Care Plusa 24/7 to request a home visit. Staff availability will be confirmed before booking.

Which areas do you cover?

Home Care Plusa provides home healthcare services across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Contact us to confirm visit availability in your specific area.

How can I book a home visit?

You can book by contacting us through WhatsApp or our website booking form.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Some services, including injections and IV drips, require a doctor's prescription and appropriate clinical assessment.

How can I confirm service charges?

Please contact our team on WhatsApp to confirm the service charges and availability before booking.
